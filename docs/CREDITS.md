# Credits

Original diagrams in `public/diagrams/` were drawn for this handbook. They are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, the same license as the educational text. See `LICENSE-CONTENT`. They are schematic. They are not copies or traces of figures from the cited guidelines, papers, or manuals.

Each page caption cites the passage the drawing follows, with the handbook's `[^source-id|locator]` marker. The English locator is the heading in that source.

No photograph is used. A stock or Wikimedia photograph was not a clearer way to show these comparisons than a diagram drawn from the cited passage, and a medical photograph would have needed a file-page license check that still would not have carried the handbook's labels.

## Diagrams

### public/diagrams/bcs.svg

- Pages: `guide/food-and-body` in zh-TW, en, zh-CN, and ja
- Shows: 9-point cat body condition, scores 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9, from the side and from above. Score 5 has a double frame.
- Source: WSAVA Body Condition Score chart for cats (2025), Body Condition Score; WSAVA Nutritional Assessment Guidelines (2011), Condition scoring; 2021 AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage Guidelines, Young adult cats
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Author: Cat Care Handbook contributors

### public/diagrams/litter-setup.svg

- Pages: `guide/litter` in all four languages
- Shows: two separated boxes with food and water apart, a box marked 1.5× a cat with a low front, and a crossed panel for a box smaller than the cat, a high-sided box, food or water against a box, and two boxes touching
- Source: AAFP and ISFM Guidelines for Diagnosing and Solving House-Soiling Behavior in Cats (2014), Designing the optimal litter box; 2021 AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage Guidelines, General litter box considerations; AAFP and ISFM Feline Environmental Needs Guidelines (2013), Pillar 2
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Author: Cat Care Handbook contributors

### public/diagrams/grimace-scale.svg

- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Author: Cat Care Handbook contributors
