## How to join

Email is not collected in this file. Open a GitHub issue with the "Vet review offer" template and include:

- your name as you want it shown on a page
- your veterinary qualification and the region where you are licensed
- which body systems you are willing to review
- a public professional page (clinic, college, or register) that a reader can check

A maintainer adds you to the table after that check. Review is voluntary. Reviewing a page does not make you the author, and it does not create a veterinary-client relationship with readers.

Setting `status: vet-reviewed` without a matching row in this file will be reverted.

## 審核獸醫（繁體中文）

目前沒有審核獸醫。

想加入的獸醫請開一個標題為 `Reviewer: 姓名` 的 GitHub issue，寫上要顯示的名字、執業資格與地區、願意審哪些系統，以及讀者能核對的公開專業頁面。維護者核對後才會把名字放進上表。

沒有出現在這份名單上的人，不能被寫進 `reviewed_by`，頁面也不能標成獸醫已審。
