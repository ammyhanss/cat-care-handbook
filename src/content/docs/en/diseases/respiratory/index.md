This group holds diseases of the nose, airways, and lungs. [Upper respiratory infection](./uri/) is a draft. It will cover feline herpesvirus and calicivirus. The body text is not written.

Sneezing and a runny nose are not automatically an emergency. Open-mouth breathing, fast or labored breathing at rest, or a change in gum color belongs on [See a vet now](../../urgent/). Fluid in the chest can come from FIP or heart disease. An owner cannot tell those apart at home.
