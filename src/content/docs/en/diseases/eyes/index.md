---

Conjunctivitis, corneal ulcers, and uveitis will sit in this group. There is no disease page in this version.

An eye that suddenly closes, clouds, changes color, or that the cat keeps pawing needs a prompt visit. Do not use human eye drops. Some ingredients injure the cornea. Eye changes can also be part of [FIP](../infectious/fip/) or an upper-respiratory virus. Sudden poor vision together with other illness belongs on [See a vet now](../../urgent/).
