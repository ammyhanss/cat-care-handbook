This group holds infectious diseases. [Feline infectious peritonitis](./fip/) is written. Panleukopenia, FeLV, and FIV do not have their own pages yet.

Finding coronavirus in blood does not diagnose FIP. Labored breathing, seizures, or almost no food for a day belong on [See a vet now](../../urgent/). This site does not give antiviral doses.
