This group holds kidney and lower urinary tract disease. [Chronic kidney disease](./ckd/) and [FLUTD and urethral obstruction](./flutd/) are drafts.

A male cat that visits the box repeatedly and passes almost no urine should go to [See a vet now](../../urgent/) before either draft is finished. Drinking more, urinating more, or a slow loss of weight needs blood and urine tests. Water intake alone is not a diagnosis.
