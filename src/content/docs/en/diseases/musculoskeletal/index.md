---

Degenerative joint disease and fractures will sit in this group. There is no disease page in this version.

Hind legs that become weak, cold, or dragging over minutes to hours are not ordinary arthritis to watch until the next day. Use [See a vet now](../../urgent/). A slower change, such as less jumping or stiff joints, can be booked as a visit. Do not start a human anti-inflammatory.
