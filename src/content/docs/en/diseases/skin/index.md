---

Ringworm, fleas, ear mites, and allergic skin disease will sit in this group. There is no disease page in this version.

Itch, hair loss, or ear debris needs a veterinarian before treatment. Do not apply a dog flea spot-on to a cat. Some of those products contain permethrin and can cause tremors and seizures. If exposure has already happened, use [See a vet now](../../urgent/) and [Toxicology and emergency](../toxicology/).
