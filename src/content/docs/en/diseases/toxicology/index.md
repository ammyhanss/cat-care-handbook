---
title: Toxicology and emergency
description: "Starting point for poisoning and other emergencies. Call immediately. Do not induce vomiting yourself."
---

Lilies, drugs, antifreeze, rodenticide, and foreign bodies will sit in this group. Individual pages are not written. The actions that cannot wait are already on [See a vet now](../../urgent/).

If a cat may have eaten or licked a toxin, call a veterinarian now and bring the package or a photo of the plant. Do not induce vomiting, and do not give milk, oil, or a human antidote. A dog flea product that contains permethrin is an emergency if it gets on a cat. The next step depends on what was ingested. This site does not give antidote doses.
