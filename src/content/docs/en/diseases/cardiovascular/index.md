---
title: Cardiovascular
description: "No heart-disease page in this version. Sudden hind-limb paralysis or labored breathing is an emergency."
---

Cardiomyopathy, heart failure, and arterial blood clots will sit in this group. There is no disease page in this version.

Sudden weak, cold, or dragging hind legs, and labored or open-mouth breathing at rest, belong on [See a vet now](../../urgent/). Do not wait for a page to be written, and do not give human heart or pain drugs.
